## What a Butuan business website should include

A useful site for an Agusan del Norte business is not a 20-page brochure. It is a clear offer and a way to contact you.

**Home:** what you do, who you serve (Butuan City and nearby), and a button to call or message.

**Services:** short pages for each offer. If you do roofing, detailing, catering, or tutoring, name those services the same way customers search.

**About:** a real person in Butuan. Local trust still matters here.

**Contact:** phone, Facebook or Messenger, map pin, and a form. Include barangay or landmark if it helps people find you.

## Local SEO for Butuan City and Agusan del Norte

Building the site is half the work. Getting found is the other half.

Local SEO for Butuan usually means:

- Google Business Profile set to the right category and city
- consistent name, address, and phone on the site and listings
- service pages that mention Butuan City, Agusan del Norte, and nearby towns when they are truly relevant
- photos of real work, not only stock images
- reviews from actual customers

If you serve the whole city plus Cabadbaran or Nasipit, say so. Do not list every municipality in Mindanao. That looks fake and does not help rankings.

## How to hire a web developer in Butuan City

Ask these before you pay a deposit:

1. Can I see live websites, not only mockups?
2. Will I be able to update text and photos later?
3. Is the site mobile-first? Most Butuan traffic is on phones.
4. Do you set up Google Business Profile and basic on-page SEO?
5. What happens after launch (updates, backups, small edits)?

If the answer to those is unclear, keep looking. A cheap site that Google never shows is not cheap.

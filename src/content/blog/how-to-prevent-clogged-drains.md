Clogged drains are one of the most common — and most preventable — plumbing problems homeowners face. Here are five simple steps you can take to keep your drains clear.

## 1. Use Drain Screens

Place mesh drain screens over all your drains to catch hair, food particles, and other debris before they enter your pipes. They cost a few dollars and can save you hundreds in plumbing bills.

## 2. Don't Pour Grease Down the Drain

Cooking grease solidifies as it cools, building up inside your pipes over time. Instead, let grease cool in a container and dispose of it in the trash.

## 3. Run Hot Water After Each Use

After using your kitchen sink, run hot water for 30 seconds to help flush any residue through the pipes.

## 4. Clean Your Drains Monthly

Pour a mixture of baking soda and vinegar down your drains once a month, followed by hot water. This natural solution helps break down buildup without harsh chemicals.

## 5. Schedule Annual Drain Maintenance

Professional drain cleaning once a year can prevent major blockages and extend the life of your plumbing system. It's a small investment that pays for itself.

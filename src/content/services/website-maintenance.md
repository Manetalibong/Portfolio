Launch is not the finish line. Plugins break, content goes stale, backups get skipped, and small issues pile up until the site feels neglected.

I provide ongoing website maintenance so your site stays updated, backed up, secure, and current after launch.

## Who this is for

Maintenance is for anyone with a live site, especially [WordPress](../wordpress-development/) installs that depend on themes and plugins.

It is also useful if you want small content edits, new photos, or seasonal updates without starting a new project every time.

## What you get

Typical work includes core, plugin, and theme updates, content changes, backups, security checks, and performance monitoring.

When something breaks, you have a direct line for technical support instead of guessing which plugin caused the error.

## How it works

We agree on what you need: update cadence, backup routine, and what counts as a simple content change versus a larger task.

I keep the site healthy in the background and handle the edits you send. That keeps marketing and SEO work from sitting on an unstable install.

## Why it matters

Outdated software is a common way sites get hacked or slowed down. A maintained site is safer, faster, and easier to improve with [SEO](../search-engine-optimization/) or new [landing pages](../landing-page-design/).

## Related services

If the site itself is the problem, maintenance will not fix a weak layout. That is a [redesign](../website-redesign/) or a new [website build](../website-design-development/). Maintenance is what keeps a good site from decaying.

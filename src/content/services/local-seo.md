When someone nearby searches for the service you offer, they should be able to find you. Local SEO is how you show up in those searches, on Google Maps, and on your Google Business Profile.

I help local and service-based businesses get found by the customers already looking in their area.

## Who this is for

Local SEO is built for businesses that serve a city, a metro area, or a set of nearby communities. Contractors, shops, clinics, and other location-based companies see the most value.

If most of your customers come from "near me" searches or from Maps, this work matters more than generic national SEO.

## What you get

I optimize your Google Business Profile so it is complete, consistent, and built to earn calls, direction requests, and website visits.

On the website, I target local search terms, tighten on-page SEO, and strengthen location signals. Citation and NAP (name, address, phone) consistency helps Google trust that your business is real and correctly listed.

## How the work happens

First I review how you appear today: profile completeness, categories, photos, reviews, website pages, and competing listings.

Then we fix the gaps: profile setup, keyword focus, service and area pages where they help, and cleanup of inconsistent listings. The site and the profile should tell the same story.

## What local SEO is not

It is not a overnight ranking guarantee. It is structured work on the assets Google already uses to decide who to show for local intent.

Results depend on competition, review activity, and how well the website supports the profile. Pairing local SEO with a strong [website](../website-design-development/) makes the click after the search more likely to convert.

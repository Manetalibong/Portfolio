## Leak Detection Services

Hidden leaks can cause thousands of dollars in damage. Our advanced leak detection technology pinpoints the exact location of leaks without unnecessary digging or demolition.

### Types of Leaks We Detect

- **Slab leaks** beneath your foundation
- **Underground pipe leaks** in your yard
- **Wall and ceiling leaks** from hidden pipes
- **Pool and spa leaks**

### Our Detection Technology

We use a combination of acoustic listening devices, thermal imaging cameras, and pressure testing to locate leaks with precision. Once found, we'll provide a clear explanation of the issue and a written estimate for repairs.

Don't wait for a small leak to become a big problem. Call us today.

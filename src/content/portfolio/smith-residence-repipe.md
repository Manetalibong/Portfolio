## Project Overview

The Smith family was experiencing low water pressure and discolored water throughout their 1970s ranch-style home. After inspection, we discovered severely corroded galvanized steel pipes that needed full replacement.

### The Challenge

- Original galvanized pipes throughout the entire home
- Limited access in some wall cavities
- Family needed to remain in the home during work

### Our Solution

We completed a full copper repipe of the home in just three days, minimizing disruption to the family. The result was dramatically improved water pressure and clean, clear water throughout the house.

### Results

- Water pressure increased by 40%
- Zero leaks since completion
- Home value increased with modern plumbing

## What local SEO means for Philippine businesses

Local SEO is how you show up when someone nearby types your service plus a city, or opens Google Maps. Typical searches look like:

- clinic near me
- web designer Surigao del Norte
- gumawa ng website Cebu
- [your trade] + Manila, Cebu, Davao, CDO, Butuan, or a town name

A useful **local SEO PH company** style engagement (without the agency layers) covers three assets that must agree:

1. **Google Business Profile** (categories, hours, photos, services, posts)
2. **Website pages** that target the services and cities you actually serve
3. **NAP consistency** (name, address, phone) across the site, profile, and major directories

I do not sell fake “page 1 in 7 days” guarantees. I sell the same foundation a serious **local SEO agency** would start with, scoped for SMEs, clinics, shops, schools, and service brands across the Philippines.

## FAQ

**Is local SEO the same as SEO?**  
Related, not identical. Local SEO prioritizes Maps and city intent. Broader [search engine optimization](../services/search-engine-optimization/) covers national or informational queries.

**Do I need a Manila agency?**  
Only if you want a large team on retainer. Many Philippine SMEs do better with a direct freelancer who can fix the profile and the site.

**Can you help if I only have Facebook?**  
Yes, but Maps still wants a website or at least a complete profile. A small site usually converts better than a Messenger-only funnel.

## What a prenup session is for

The photos become save-the-dates, invitation layouts, and the photobooth or entrance AV. Families also keep them as the “before” chapter. A useful Butuan prenup usually includes:

- Two or three outfit changes
- A main location plus a backup if rain hits
- Couple portraits, walking frames, and a few detail shots (rings, bouquet, shoes)
- Optional short video clips for a teaser

If you want motion, say so. A photographer with video (and drone when the location allows it) keeps the teaser and stills in one style.

## Locations around Butuan and Caraga

I plan locations around the look you want, not a random pin:

- Downtown / poblacion streets and heritage corners in Butuan City
- Garden and riverside spots in Agusan del Norte
- Cabadbaran City and Nasipit when you want a quieter backdrop
- Buenavista and Las Nieves for more open, provincial frames
- Destination looks elsewhere in the Caraga Region when travel is in the quote

Tell me if you already have a venue, a stylist, or a florist. I will work around their timeline so you are not rushing between hair, makeup, and golden hour.

## How the shoot runs

**Plan.** You send mood references (clean, cinematic, candid, or classic) and outfit colors. I reply with a location shortlist and a call time.

**Shoot.** We move with enough time for stills. If video is included, clips are captured between portrait sets so you are not standing still for an hour.

**Deliver.** You get a preview set, then the full gallery. Files are sized for print and for social.

## What affects the prenup quote

Prenup rates in Butuan move with:

- Hours on location
- Number of sites (one garden vs downtown plus a second stop)
- Stylist and florist coordination
- Video teaser
- Drone, when the site allows it
- Travel to Cabadbaran, Nasipit, Buenavista, Las Nieves, or farther Caraga towns

## How a project usually runs

**Consult.** You tell me what the business does, who you serve, and whether you already have photos, a Facebook page, or an old site. I reply with a recommended page list and a quote.

**Design.** We agree on layout, colors, and the offer that should sit above the fold. The goal is a site that makes a first-time visitor feel they found a real local business, not a placeholder.

**Build.** I develop the pages, forms, mobile layout, and the technical basics search engines need: headings, titles, and clean URLs.

**Launch.** We check the site on a phone, connect the domain, and set up the contact paths you actually use: call, email, Messenger, or a form.

**Support.** After launch you can keep a [maintenance](../services/website-maintenance/) plan for updates, backups, and small edits, or request help only when something changes.

## What “responsive” means in practice

A **responsive website design in Butuan City** is not a slogan. Most of your visitors will tap with a thumb on a small screen, often on mobile data. Buttons need to be large enough. Text needs to stay readable. Click-to-call should be obvious. Photo galleries should not drag the page down.

That is also why design and development stay together on this hub. A pretty mockup that cannot be maintained, or a cheap theme that breaks on Android, will not help the business.

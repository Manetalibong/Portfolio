## What a Cebu City website should include

Cebu users have seen a lot of templates. The site has to do more than look “modern.”

**Home.** What you sell in Cebu, a proof line, and one primary action (book, call, or inquire).

**Services or packages.** Unique URLs. Hotels, clinics, schools, logistics firms, and cafes lose money when five offers share one vague block.

**About.** Who runs the business. A real Cebu or Philippine story beats a stock skyline.

**Work, rooms, menu, or fleet.** Photos shot on site. Heavy sliders that freeze mid-range Androids will cost you the next tap.

**Contact.** Phone, email, map, and hours. Keep NAP identical on Google Business Profile.

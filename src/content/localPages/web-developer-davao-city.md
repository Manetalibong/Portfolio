## What a Davao City website should include

**Home.** One sentence for what you do in Davao, who you serve, and a primary button (call, Messenger, or quote).

**Service pages.** Separate URLs. A detailing shop, clinic, contractor, tutoring brand, or hotel should not hide five offers in one paragraph.

**About.** A face and a Davao (or Mindanao) story. Trust still matters here.

**Work or menu.** Photos of actual jobs, rooms, plates, or storefronts. Stock skylines from another country hurt more than they help.

**Contact.** Number, map, hours, parking or landmark notes. Align this with Google Business Profile so NAP stays consistent.

## Freelance flexibility vs a typical agency

Agencies can be a good fit for huge brands with many stakeholders. Most Butuan and Caraga businesses are not that. They need a capable **freelance web developer in Butuan** who can:

You also avoid paying extra layers of project management for a five-page site. The time goes into design, development, and making the site usable on a phone.

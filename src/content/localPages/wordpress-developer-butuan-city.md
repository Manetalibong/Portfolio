## Why WordPress still makes sense here

WordPress is widely used because the admin is familiar, hosting options are plenty, and you are not locked into a builder that disappears when a subscription lapses.

For a non-technical owner that means:

- You can update headlines, hours, and service blurbs yourself
- You can add blog posts when you have something useful to say locally
- You can grow later with a shop, extra landing pages, or a simple membership area
- You are not paying per tiny text change for the life of the site

The plugin ecosystem is large. That is a benefit and a risk. I pick plugins that earn their place, then keep the install lean so the site stays fast on mobile data.

## A beautiful site nobody finds is a wasted investment

You pay for photos, copy, and a domain. If Google cannot understand the pages, or if they target the wrong phrases, the site sits on page two while a competitor with a simpler page ranks for “web designer Butuan City,” “clinic near me,” or whatever your customers type.

**Web design and SEO in the Philippines** should start with intent:

- Which services should rank in Butuan and nearby towns
- Which page is the canonical home for each offer
- How Google Business Profile, the website, and NAP details stay consistent

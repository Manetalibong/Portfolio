**School website design in the Philippines** and **church website design in the Philippines** look similar from a distance: events, people, and trust. The jobs they have to do are different. Parents hunting enrollment dates should not land on a livestream player. A church member checking Sunday schedule should not wade through an admissions portal.

I am Mane Talibong. I design both, then train whoever will actually log in after I leave: a registrar, a secretary, a volunteer, or a campus paper editor.

## School sites: admissions, faculty, calendar, portals

A workable school site usually needs:

- Clear admissions or enrollment information with dates and requirements
- Faculty or department pages that stay accurate when people change roles
- A calendar or announcements stream for holidays, exams, and events
- Links to existing portals (enrollment systems, LMS, email) instead of reinventing them
- Contact paths for registrar, principal, or campus offices

Tone stays professional. Photos should look like your campus, not stock classrooms from another country.

I bring relevant campus publication experience. I have served as Editor in Chief of The Watch Guild (TWG) while studying BSIS at PECIT in Butuan City. That is useful when a school site also needs a news or student-publication section that staff can update without breaking layout.

## Church sites: schedule, sermons, giving

A church or ministry site is a different brief:

- Service or mass schedules, including special Holy Week or simbang gabi notes
- Sermon, reflection, or livestream links if you already publish them
- Location and parking notes for first-time visitors
- Giving or donation information presented carefully, with the channels you actually use
- Ministry pages (youth, music, outreach) only if someone will maintain them

The goal is welcome and clarity, not a marketing landing page that feels out of place.

## Easy updates for non-technical staff

Both schools and churches fail when only the vendor can change a date. I prefer WordPress with a simple editor and a short handbook: how to add an announcement, how to replace a banner, how not to paste from Word with hidden formatting.

## Why a provincial-capital site is a bit different

Offices and businesses here often serve people coming in from other municipalities. Your website should make office hours, location, and contact paths obvious. If you work with provincial programs, cooperatives, or retail that serves the capital, the site should not assume the visitor already knows Cabadbaran the way a local does.
